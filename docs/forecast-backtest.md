# Price forecast backtest (2026-11-15)

Rolling-origin test: 4 origins from 2025-11-17 to 2026-08-17, each forecasting the next 13 weeks of anchors 1-12 weeks ahead, trained only on what was known before the origin. 3,046,512 forecasts over 8,930 price lines (179 crops).

**Ratio** = forecast error / error of "the price stays the same" (mean |ln actual/forecast|; below 100% beats it; shown in the app at 90% or less, with the 90% upper bound under 100%). **Band** = share of outcomes inside the 10-90% band (target 80%; shown at 75-85%). **App seasonal** = the app's seasonal method at 7-12 weeks (best blend), where it was tested.

Passed: 46 crops. Overall ratio: 1-2 wk 99%, 3-6 wk 88%, 7-12 wk 79%; band holds 80%.
